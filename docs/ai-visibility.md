## Sources opened and citation advantages

Counts: answers citing exact URLs, excluding localized variants. Vendor claims are untested.

- [Spotify Jam][s8] (7): authoritative device instructions, Premium prerequisites and host controls. [YouTube collaboration][s18] (3) documents voting with signed-in accounts; not an account-free equivalent.
- [Jamocracy][s13], [Social Jukebox][s12], [Schwifty][s15] (3 each): comparison table, explicit pricing/device requirements, and FAQ/feature sections respectively. [Jukestar][s7] (3) has a September 2022 shutdown notice explaining negative mentions.
- VoteTheDJ, The Jukebox App, Festify, MUSIKWUNSCHAPP, Muwish, Juqed and Jukr’s Play listing (2 each; links above) offer concrete steps, provider/device distinctions or guest requirements. Muwish explicitly pauses new activations. Juqed separates host platforms; MUSIKWUNSCHAPP uses German category language.
- SpecialRequest’s house-party page matches an exact use case. Rekwest’s cited Play listing shows 10K+ downloads, a 16 October 2026 update and developer-claimed 220,000 users: distinct metrics, not verified adoption.

No forums, YouTube videos, independent listicles or general directories were cited. Formats suggest opportunities, not ranking causes.
