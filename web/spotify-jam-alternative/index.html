When Jam is enough

If you already use Premium and everyone wants to listen through Spotify, Jam provides QR invitations, a shared queue and host controls. Choose based on device and account requirements and how you want guests to participate.

Try SongVoter

Download the Android APK, create a party and enable your music sources. Keep the host app open. Guests scan the QR, search for songs and heart them. Each profile can have up to 30 favourites sharing its voting weight.
