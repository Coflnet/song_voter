A few things before
the first track.

Is SongVoter free?

Yes. SongVoter is free to use.

Does everyone need an app?

Only the host needs the Android app. Guests join with any modern phone browser. Keep the host app open so playback and the party QR stay visible.

How do YouTube and Spotify work?

The host app plays YouTube videos on screen. Spotify playback uses the installed Spotify app and the host’s Premium account. Guests can add songs from either platform; the queue plays the sources the host has enabled.

Will I keep my favourites?

Yes. SongVoter remembers your anonymous profile on this browser or device. Your favourites come with you when you join another party. Clearing browser data or changing devices starts a new profile.

Can I host on an iPhone?

You can join from your iPhone browser today. Hosting is available on Android first; an iOS host app is not available yet.
