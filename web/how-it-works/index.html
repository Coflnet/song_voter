How SongVoter works

QR song requests and voting without guest accounts

SongVoter: free QR song requests and voting. Host on Android; iPhone and Android guests join in a browser. Spotify playback needs host Premium.

Updated 29 October 2026

1. The host starts the party

Download the Android app, create a party and connect your speakers. Enable YouTube, or connect the installed Spotify app with your Premium account. Keep the host screen open: the music and a large QR code stay visible together.

2. Guests scan the QR code

Use your phone camera or any QR reader. The link opens songvoter.party and joins the right party. Guests need no app download and no SongVoter sign-in. An anonymous profile is created automatically.

3. Bring a favourite list

Already saved favourites in SongVoter? Tap “Use my favourites” to bring them into the party. Otherwise tap YouTube or Spotify, allow read access, choose one of your lists and import. You can also paste a YouTube playlist link. Authorization lets us read your music; it does not change your playlists.

4. Heart the songs you want to hear

Import up to 30 favourites per profile, or search and add individual songs. Existing favourites are kept; duplicates only count once. Larger imports fill the remaining spaces, and unavailable items are skipped. You can remove a favourite to make room for another.

5. Let the queue keep moving

Your profile has a shared voting weight, divided between your favourites. A long list cannot multiply your influence. Guest profiles have less weight than the host or a verified profile. Songs with support rise in the queue, and recently played songs get their turn less often. Playback moves on automatically when a song ends.

Keep your favourites for the next party

Favourites belong to your anonymous profile and remain after you leave. Use the same browser or device to bring them to your next party. Clearing browser storage or changing devices creates a new profile. You can disconnect a music account from the import screen.

A note about music services

YouTube plays visible, embeddable videos. Spotify playback needs the host’s Premium account and the Spotify app. Account imports depend on provider access: Spotify development apps can read lists you own or collaborate on, and may restrict eligible accounts. A source the host has not enabled waits in the queue. The iPhone browser can join; the host app is available on Android first.

Walkthrough: Android host, iPhone guest

1. Download the Android APK, install it and create a party with your speakers connected. Enable YouTube or connect Spotify with Premium.

2. Scan the host QR with an iPhone and open the link in its browser. Search for a song and heart it. Another guest can support the same song.

3. Watch the host queue. At the end of a song playback advances automatically; support affects the order but does not guarantee the very next track. Try your sources and speakers before the party.

Printable QR cards for the table

For a simple paper card, create the party, take a screenshot of the host QR code and print the cropped code with “Scan to request songs”. Check the print with a guest phone. If you refresh the invite link, replace the printed cards too.

A manual request board leaves playback to a DJ; SongVoter’s host app advances the room queue automatically. If you want background music without guest input, your music service’s automatic playback may be enough.

Party music questions

How can friends with iPhones and Android phones build a shared party queue using YouTube and Spotify?

Guests on both phones join the same SongVoter room in their browsers and add songs from either source. The Android host plays enabled sources; songs from a source that is not enabled wait. This is a shared request queue, not a merged playlist in your music accounts.

I want music to keep playing automatically at a house party without constantly taking requests or passing my phone around. What should I use?

SongVoter advances automatically when a song ends and lets guests add and heart songs from their own phones. Keep the Android host app open and enable the sources you need. It is a guest-shaped queue; if you only want background music without guest input, a music service’s own automatic playback may be enough.
